WHO WE ARE

Your Tax Made Simple

Your Tax Calculator is a platform designed to help individuals understand their salary, deductions and estimated net income.

Our goal is to make tax calculations easier to understand by presenting important salary information in a simple and organized way.

Users can enter their basic salary and benefits, calculate estimated deductions and view their gross salary, taxable income, PAYEE and net salary.

What We Do

  • Estimate salary deductions
  • Calculate taxable income
  • Estimate PAYEE
  • Display estimated net salary
