GET TO KNOW US

Learn more about the people and purpose behind Your Tax Calculator.

Built to Make Tax Easier

Your Tax Calculator was created with the goal of making salary and tax calculations easier to understand.

We believe that understanding your salary, deductions and estimated net income should not be complicated. Our platform is designed to present this information in a simple, clear and organized way.

What We Care About

Simplicity

We aim to make salary and tax calculations easy to understand.

Clarity

We present tax-related information in a clear and organized format.

Accessibility

We want everyone to have access to simple tools for understanding their salary and deductions.

Our Journey

Your Tax Calculator started as a simple idea: create a tool that allows users to enter their salary and benefits and quickly understand their gross salary, deductions, PAYEE and estimated net salary.

As the platform grows, we aim to continue adding useful features and services that make tax-related information easier to access and understand.
